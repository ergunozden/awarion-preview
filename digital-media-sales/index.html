Frequently Asked Questions

Reselling programmatic digital-out-of-home with Awarion, answered.

Awarion's Junction platform provides sub-admin level management, meaning a reseller or a sales house looking to develop its market with DOOH media owners and advertisers can access both sides within a single view. Awarion provides its platform and second-line technical support, while the sales partner can focus on business development and benefit from a revenue-share model.
This partnership is ideal for ad networks and digital sales houses that want to sell programmatic DOOH without building internal software. They may be already active in their country with existing agency and advertiser relationships from different media. The partner retains commercial control over both represented publishers and the brand advertisers they serve. Custom commercial frameworks can be set up that would be optimal for the market.
Partners receive sub-admin access to manage referred publishers, adjust screen metadata, and build detailed proposals through the interactive planner that can be shared directly with clients. After initial training sessions, partners can manage campaigns directly, grant clients self-service access, and separately use Awarion's CreativeLab services for dynamic creative.
Awarion removes development overhead by providing a deployed SSP, DSP, planner, reporting suite, and CreativeLab production capacity. The architecture includes aggregator-grade price management, interactive proposal generation, and granular sub-user permissions at competitive fee structures.
