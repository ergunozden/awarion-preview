Frequently Asked Questions

Buying programmatic digital-out-of-home with Awarion Junction, answered.

Awarion Junction is a programmatic advertising platform that connects brands and agencies directly to roughly 30,000 digital-out-of-home frames. Within this unified interface, buyers select screens, receive automated budget recommendations, manage placements, deploy conditional targeting, and analyze screen-level reporting.
Yes, Junction is also a demand-side platform (DSP) built from the ground up exclusively for programmatic DOOH rather than web display/video only. The platform supports both self-service and managed-service buying options under contract, while external DSP and SSP integrations are continually evaluated.
Advertisers begin buying by executing a contract and completing a brief platform onboarding training session. Users then use Junction's planner to select screens and budgets, the campaign manager to automate placements, and conditional targeting rules to optimize performance.
Awarion targets with first-party brand data, third-party socio-demographic insights, and the Live Audience API for real-time signals. Additionally, CreativeLab optimizes campaign creative to match live conditions like weather and road traffic. Additional dayparting targeting can be layered on to focus on times that matter.
Awarion measures campaign performance through real-time, individual screen-level reporting covering pacing, impressions, conditional triggers, and playout heatmaps. This data lets buyers optimize budgets mid-campaign, a programmatic approach that won the 2024 AdExchanger Award for Best Programmatic OOH Activation. Additionally, advertisers can opt-in to online retargeting, brand lift studies and footfall measurement to execute a cross-channel strategy.
